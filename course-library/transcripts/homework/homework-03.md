# Homework 3

STAT 132

1. Suppose that you observe a random sample, $X_1, \ldots, X_n$, from the normal distribution with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$. Find the distribution of
$$\frac{n(\bar{X} - \mu)^2}{\sigma^2}.$$

2. Suppose that a point $(X, Y, Z)$ is to be chosen at random in three-dimensional space, where $X$, $Y$, and $Z$ are independent random variables, each following a standard normal distribution. What is the probability that the distance from the origin to the random point will be less than 1?

3. If a random variable $X$ follows a $\chi^2$ distribution with $m$ degrees of freedom, then the distribution of $X^{1/2}$ is called a chi ($\chi$) distribution with $m$ degrees of freedom. Determine the mean of this chi distribution.

4. Suppose that $X$ has the $\chi^2$ distribution with 200 degrees of freedom. Explain why the central limit theorem can be used to determine the approximate value of $\mathbb{P}(160 < X < 240)$ and find this approximate value.

5. Let $X_1, \ldots, X_n$ be a random sample from the normal distribution with mean $\mu$ and variance $\sigma^2$. Also let $\widehat{\sigma^2}$ denote the sample variance. Find the smallest value of $n$ for which the below expression is satisfied:
$$\mathbb{P}\left(\frac{\widehat{\sigma^2}}{\sigma^2} \le 1.5\right) \ge 0.95$$
