# Homework 1

STAT 132

1. Suppose that the prior distribution of some parameter $\theta$ is a gamma distribution for which the mean is 5 and the variance is 2. Determine the prior pdf of $\theta$.

2. Suppose that the prior distribution of some parameter $\theta$ is a beta distribution for which the mean is $2/3$ and the variance is $1/48$. Determine the prior pdf of $\theta$.

3. Suppose you flip a possibly weighted coin 100 times and observe 60 heads. Write the likelihood function for $p$, the true proportion of heads resulting from this coin. Then, sketch this likelihood function over the $[0, 1]$ interval.

4. Suppose that the number of people living in a household can be modeled using a Poisson distribution with mean $\lambda$. You take a random sample of 10 households and observe household sizes $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_{10}$. Write the likelihood function for $\lambda$.

5. Suppose that the number of people living in a household can be modeled using a Poisson distribution with mean $\lambda$. You take a random sample of 10 households and observe household sizes $x_1, x_2, \ldots, x_{10}$. Your friend suggests a normal prior distribution with mean 1 and variance 10 for $\lambda$. Is this a reasonable prior distribution for this problem? Why or why not?
