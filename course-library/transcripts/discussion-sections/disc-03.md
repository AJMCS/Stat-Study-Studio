# Discussion Section 3

STAT 132, Winter 2025

1. Suppose that $X_1, \ldots, X_n$ are a random sample from the Normal distribution with unknown mean $\mu$ and unknown variance $\sigma^2$. Find the maximum likelihood estimator for $\mu$ and $\sigma^2$?

2. A random variable $X$ is modeled using the following PDF:
$$f(x|\theta) = \frac{\theta}{x^{\theta+1}} \quad \text{for } x > 1,$$
where $\theta > 0$. Suppose you observe the random sample $X_1, \ldots, X_n$. What is the MLE of $\theta$?

3. Suppose that $X_1, \ldots, X_n$ form a random sample from the uniform distribution on the interval $[\theta_1, \theta_2]$, where both $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$ are unknown ($-\infty < \theta_1 < \theta_2 < \infty$). Find the MLE of $\theta_1$ and $\theta_2$.

4. Suppose that $X_1, \ldots, X_n$ form a random sample from an exponential distribution for which the value of the parameter $\lambda$ is unknown. Determine the <u>MLE of the median</u> of the distribution.

5. Suppose that $X_1, \ldots, X_n$ are a random sample from the Normal distribution with unknown mean $\mu$ and unknown variance $\sigma^2$. Find the MLE of the 0.95 quantile of the distribution (i.e. the value of $a$ such that $\mathbb{P}(X < a) = 0.95$).
